3D Food Kiosk

3D Food Kiosk in Blender

A 3D scene of a food kiosk in a neon-lit night setting, with attention to form, atmosphere and visual composition.

ToolingBlender
Scope3D Modeling
Lighting
Scene Design

Goal

This project explores how a scene can build a strong visual presence through form, material choices and a clear nighttime atmosphere.

Execution

The kiosk was designed as a colorful food stand with a strong atmosphere. I played with tone, form, lighting and a stylized look so the scene immediately tells a story.

I experimented with modeling, texturing and composition in Blender to build a clear visual identity that matches the desired nighttime ambiance.

View the scene

The renders show the kiosk in a nighttime setting with neon lighting, warm materials and a compact retail concept that works visually at a glance.
