Cup-a-cino

Webflow webshop & React Native companion app for specialty coffee — CMS-driven products, filters and ordering.

Timeline
Jan 2025 – Jun 2025
Collaborations
Solo project
Scope
  • Webflow storefront
  • React Native app
  • CMS & API integration

Cup-a-cino is a CMS-driven e-commerce experience for specialty coffee. Webflow powers the shop and content through Collections and dynamic templates, so product pages, category pages and blog posts can be maintained without touching code. The React Native app acts as the mobile companion and uses the same content source: users can browse the full catalogue, open product detail pages with images and tasting notes, filter and sort items by origin, roast, price or tags, add products to a cart, and move through the shopping flow in a mobile-first interface.

The challenge was to keep a polished visual system while remaining maintainable. CMS-driven products bring a lot of structure behind the scenes, because variants, images, metadata and categories all need to stay consistent. The app also had to reflect the same content in a clear, performant way, which meant thinking carefully about reusable components, predictable product flows and a clean content schema that works for both the website and the app.

The project taught me how to balance content needs with e-commerce constraints and how to design components that remain flexible for future product additions.
