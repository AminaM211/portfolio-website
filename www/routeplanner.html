Project

Accessible Route Planner

Designing a user-centered route planning app focused on accessibility, clarity and inclusive navigation.

The interface was designed around clear visual hierarchy, WCAG-inspired contrast choices, accessible feedback patterns and route options that help users make more informed travel decisions.

Problem

Many route planning apps focus mainly on speed and efficiency, but accessibility needs are often treated as secondary. For users who rely on clear feedback, readable interfaces or accessible route options, this can make planning a journey stressful or unclear.

The challenge was to design a route planning experience that supports different user needs without making the interface feel complex or overwhelming.

Research & Discovery

To better understand accessibility challenges in route planning applications, I evaluated existing solutions using Nielsen's usability heuristics and accessibility guidelines such as WCAG.

The evaluation revealed several recurring issues, including unclear interface states, feedback that relied solely on color, inconsistent navigation patterns and limited support for different accessibility needs.

Key Insight

The biggest insight was that accessibility should be integrated into the core experience rather than treated as a separate feature. Small design decisions such as feedback patterns, icon usage, contrast and navigation structure often had a greater impact than dedicated accessibility settings.

Accessibility Decisions

Accessibility was considered throughout the design process rather than added afterwards. Using Nielsen's usability heuristics and accessibility guidelines as evaluation tools, I iteratively refined the interface to improve clarity, feedback and usability for a wider range of users.

01Clear Selection States

Early iterations relied primarily on color to indicate the selected transport mode. Following WCAG principles, an additional checkmark was introduced to provide a secondary visual cue and ensure that selection states remained understandable for users with color vision deficiencies.

02Visibility of System Status

Inspired by Nielsen's Visibility of System Status heuristic, users continuously receive feedback about where they are in a process. Progress indicators were added to multi-step flows such as ticket purchases, helping users understand their current step and what remains before completion.

03Faster Route Planning

To reduce cognitive effort and repetitive input, frequently used locations, recent searches and the user's current location were integrated directly into the search flow. This allows users to start planning routes more quickly while minimizing manual typing.

04Personalized Accessibility Preferences

The platform includes accessibility settings that allow users to customize the experience according to their needs. Options such as increased contrast, larger text sizes, audio feedback, vibration notifications and accessibility-focused route preferences provide greater control over how information is presented and consumed.

Wireframing

During the wireframing phase, I explored how to simplify the route planning flow into a few clear steps: selecting a transport type, entering a starting point and destination, comparing route options and following the selected route.

The wireframes helped define the structure of the main screens before adding visual design, accessibility settings and map interactions.

Branding & Visual Direction

The visual design focused on clarity, contrast and consistency. Colors were chosen with accessibility in mind, while interface states were supported by icons, labels and layout changes instead of relying on color alone.

Components such as buttons, cards, filters and route steps were designed to be easy to scan and interact with on mobile.

Finale Outcome

The final prototype presents an accessible route planning experience that helps users plan, compare and follow public transport routes with more confidence.

By combining accessible route preferences, clear feedback, readable interface components and inclusive map options, the app supports users with different needs while keeping the overall experience simple and approachable.
