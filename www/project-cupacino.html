Cup-a-cino:
Webflow website & React Native app

Een CMS-gedreven e-commerce ervaring voor specialty koffiebonen. Webflow voor de shop en content, React Native voor mobiel gebruik met live API-data.

React Native
API

Context

Cup-a-cino started as a web and app concept for specialty coffee. The goal was to build a clear, CMS-driven experience where products, blog content and ordering flow felt warm, simple and consistent across devices.

My focus was on translating the brand into a practical digital system: a responsive storefront in Webflow and a companion mobile experience in React Native.

Uitdaging

The biggest challenge was keeping the design consistent while handling CMS-driven content and app states. The shop needed to feel polished, but also stay easy to maintain and flexible enough for future product updates.

Oplossing

I designed the Webflow storefront around strong editorial layout, clear hierarchy and reusable components. This kept the shopping flow intuitive while leaving room for CMS content like product stories and blog articles.

The visual system uses soft neutrals, coffee tones and generous spacing so the brand feels calm and premium rather than crowded or overly technical.

Mijn rol

I handled the Webflow build, the content structure and the design decisions that kept the webshop readable on desktop and mobile. In the mobile app, I worked on the React Native structure and the connection between content and interface.

The project sharpened my skills in component thinking, implementation detail and balancing visual polish with maintainable front-end decisions.

Reflectie

Cup-a-cino taught me how important it is to connect content, layout and behavior into one coherent experience. The strongest part of the project was turning a concept into something that feels usable and editorial at the same time.
