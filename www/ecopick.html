Ecopick: Duurzame Mode App

Een mobiele app die duurzame alternatieven voor kledingstukken toont via AI-beeldherkenning en een ondersteunende community.

Timeline
Sept 2025 - Dec 2025
Collaborations
Solo project
Scope
  • Concept development
  • Branding
  • UX/UI Design
  • Development

Ecopick started as a concept around sustainable fashion. The app gives users a low-threshold way to discover alternatives that are better for people and the planet.

Many consumers want to shop more sustainably, but it is hard to find stylish and affordable alternatives. The market is fragmented, labels are unclear and fast fashion remains the default.

With Ecopick, users upload a photo of a clothing item and receive sustainable alternatives through AI image recognition. The concept combines second-hand platforms, sustainable brands and a community where users can share tips and discoveries.
