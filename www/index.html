I am a UX/UI designer who thinks in stories rather than screens. What drives me is creating digital experiences that are not only beautiful, but that also make people feel something. I believe design truly works when it evokes emotion while remaining clear, logical, and honest. In every project, I search for the balance between feeling and structure, between people and brand.

My strength lies in empathy and attention to detail. That's how I create interfaces that do not just function, but leave a lasting impression. I work best with music playing, a coffee within reach, and ideas that may start out a little wild but always come together beautifully.
